The simple way to enrol patients on health apps

Sign a patient up to an NHS health app in a few steps. A health app helps a patient manage a health condition, like COPD or asthma.

For GPs, NHS trusts and other NHS teams.

Log in with my Care Identity

Free to use

Made and run by the NHS.

Connected

Finds patients using NHS records. Sends each enrolment to the company that makes the app.

Safe

Checks what a patient is already on, so they are not enrolled twice.

How it works

1Log in

Log in with your NHS smartcard or Care Identity.

2Find the patient

Use their NHS number. Or use their name and date of birth.

3Check what they are already on

See the health apps the patient already uses. A patient can only be on each app once.

Who can use this service

You can use this service if you work in an NHS team that looks after patients and is a participant in NHS HealthStore. This includes GP surgeries, secondary care settings and NHS trusts.

You need an NHS smartcard or Care Identity to log in. If you do not have one, ask your local smartcard team.
