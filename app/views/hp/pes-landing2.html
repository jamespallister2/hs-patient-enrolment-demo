The simple way to enrol patients onto health apps

Sign a patient up to a health app and invite them via the NHS App in just a few steps. For people working in teams participating in NHS HealthStore, in primary and secondary care settings

An NHS HealthStore service

Log in with my Care Identity

Initiates NHS App invitation

Go from clinical discussion to initiating the NHS App invite message in a few basic steps

Integrated with PDS

Finds patients using NHS records. Our service alerts the provider and sets up the patient's account

Safety netting built in

Checks what a patient is already on, so they are not enrolled twice.

How it works

1Log in

Log in with your NHS smartcard or Care Identity.

2Find the patient

Use their NHS number. Or use their name and date of birth.

3Check what they are already on

See the health apps the patient already uses. A patient can only be on each app once.

Who can use this service

You can use this service if you work in an NHS team that is a participant in NHS HealthStore. This includes GP surgeries and NHS trusts.

You need an NHS smartcard or Care Identity to log in. If you do not have one, ask your local smartcard team.
